Web App

Orbit — E-commerce Dashboard

An analytics dashboard that helps sellers track performance at a glance.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
8 weeks
Platform
Web (desktop-first)
Tools
Figma, Apache ECharts, Notion
Orbit — cover

The Problem

Sellers had to cross-reference three separate reports just to answer "how is my store doing today," and most gave up before finding the number they needed.

The existing reports were technically accurate but built around what the database could export, not what a seller actually checks first each morning.

Process

Shadowed 5 sellers through their morning routine, audited the existing reports line by line, and prototyped a single glance-view that combined revenue, inventory, and traffic in one place.

The Solution

One dashboard home surfaces the four metrics sellers checked daily first, with drill-down available on demand instead of shown by default.

Results

Sellers found what they needed faster, and support stopped fielding the same "where do I find X" question on repeat.

−58%Support tickets about finding data
3×Faster time-to-insight in testing
91%Pilot sellers preferred it
