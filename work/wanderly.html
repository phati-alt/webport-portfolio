Mobile App

Wanderly — Travel Booking App

Simplifying trip planning with a delightful, story-driven booking flow.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
12 weeks
Platform
iOS / Android
Tools
Figma, FigJam, UserTesting
Wanderly — cover

The Problem

The booking flow asked for every trip detail up front, so undecided users — the majority — bounced before ever seeing a price.

Analytics showed most sessions started with "just browsing," not "ready to book," but the product only had one flow for both.

Process

Mapped the emotional arc of trip planning from "just browsing" to "ready to book," then tested a progressive-disclosure flow with 10 participants at each stage of that arc.

The Solution

Split booking into a lightweight "explore" mode with flexible dates, and a separate "book" mode that only asks for detail once a trip is actually chosen.

Results

Splitting the flow in two turned more browsers into bookers, and got them there faster once they'd decided.

+41%Browse-to-book conversion
−27%Time to first booking
4.7/5Post-task satisfaction
