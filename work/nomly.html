Mobile App

Nomly — Food Delivery App

Reducing checkout drop-off with a streamlined ordering experience.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
6 weeks
Platform
iOS / Android
Tools
Figma, Maze, FigJam
Nomly — cover

The Problem

Checkout had 5 screens, and users kept getting dropped by a late "delivery unavailable" error — losing an order they'd already spent time building.

Only six weeks were available before the next release window, so the fix had to reuse the existing availability API rather than wait on a backend change.

Process

Ran a checkout teardown against 12 competitor apps, moved availability checks earlier in the flow, and tested a condensed two-screen checkout with 9 participants.

The Solution

Delivery-radius and time checks now run the moment an item is added to the cart, not at the final step, and checkout itself condensed down to address and payment.

Results

Moving the failure point earlier meant fewer people hit it after investing time in an order, and checkout itself simply had less to get through.

−23%Checkout abandonment
5→2Checkout screens
96%Task completion (n=9)
