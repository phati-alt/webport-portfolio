Web App

Vitalis — Healthcare Portal

Making patient records and appointments accessible for every age group.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
14 weeks
Platform
Web (responsive)
Tools
Figma, axe DevTools, UserTesting
Vitalis — cover

The Problem

Older patients couldn't find their own lab results or upcoming appointments without calling the clinic — a "self-service" portal that wasn't self-service at all for the people who used it most.

The portal also had to meet WCAG 2.1 AA by the end of the project, not just read as accessible — an explicit compliance requirement from the client.

Process

Usability tested with participants aged 55–78, audited every core flow against WCAG 2.1 AA, and simplified navigation from 6 top-level sections down to 3.

The Solution

Plain-language labels, larger touch targets, and a single "What's next" home view surfacing the next appointment and any new results first, ahead of everything else.

Results

The portal hit its accessibility target, and the clinic's phones got quieter — the clearest sign patients were finding things on their own.
