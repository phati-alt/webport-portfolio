Mobile App

Finza — Fintech Mobile App

Redesigning a personal finance app to simplify budgeting and boost daily engagement.

Role
Lead UX/UI Designer
Timeline
10 weeks
Platform
iOS / Android
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Maze
Finza — cover

The Problem

Most users abandoned budgeting within the first week — setup demanded too much manual entry and gave no feedback until the end of the month.

The team had ten weeks and an existing bank-sync integration to build on, so the fix had to work within that data, not around it.

Process

Ran remote usability sessions with 8 existing users, mapped the budget-setup flow end to end, and tested a lighter "quick categories" version as a clickable prototype before touching production code.

The Solution

Onboarding now auto-categorizes transactions straight from the bank sync instead of asking users to sort them, and a weekly progress ring on the home screen replaced the old monthly-only summary.

Results

Retention and setup speed both moved in testing, and the usability score held up across a second round with new participants.
